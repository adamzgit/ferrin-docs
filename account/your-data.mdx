You can leave Ferrin anytime and request account deletion. When you do, Ferrin securely deletes your data, except where it's legally required to keep it.

For details on what Ferrin collects and how it's handled, see our [privacy policy](https://www.ferrin.app/privacy). For privacy questions or requests, email [privacy@ferrin.app](mailto:privacy@ferrin.app).
