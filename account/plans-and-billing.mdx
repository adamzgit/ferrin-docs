Ferrin has a free plan with no time limit, and paid plans that add the cloud, so you can use Ferrin on your Mac and your iPhone with your tasks in sync. See what each plan includes on the [pricing page](https://www.ferrin.app/pricing).

## Free

Free runs on one device, your Mac or your iPhone, and your tasks stay on it. It has no time limit.

## The free trial

Paid plans start with a 14-day free trial, for new subscribers only: one trial per person, and one per team.

- You get everything in the plan during the trial, and you can cancel any time.
- When the trial ends, your plan starts and the first payment is taken, unless you cancel first.
- If you subscribed through the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

## Monthly or yearly

Pay monthly, or pay once a year and get two months free: 12 months for the price of 10. Paid plans renew automatically until you cancel.

## Change your plan

- **Upgrade** whenever you like. The new features open straight away, and you pay only the difference for the rest of your current period.
- **Downgrade** and the change takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.

## Cancel

- **App Store:** in your device's subscription settings.
- **Paid directly:** in Ferrin's settings.

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, then moves to Free.

## Moving to Free

Nothing you made is lost:

- **You choose a device to keep,** your Mac or your iPhone, and everything is saved there.
- **Your other device** can still show your tasks, read only.
- **We keep your cloud copy for 90 days.** Upgrade within that time and everything syncs again. After that it's deleted, and we email you a week before.
- **Sharing stops,** and connectors other than Apple Calendar and Apple Mail are disconnected.

## Refunds

- **App Store purchases:** Apple handles refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Yearly plans paid directly:** ask within 14 days of the first yearly charge for a full refund.
- **Monthly plans paid directly:** we don't refund part of a month, but you won't be charged again once you cancel.

To ask for a refund, [email us](https://www.ferrin.app/api/contact?to=hello) from the address on your account. The full details are in our [terms of service](https://www.ferrin.app/terms).
