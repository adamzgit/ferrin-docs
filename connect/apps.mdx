A connector lets Ferrin use what's already in another app when it plans: your meetings, the email you flagged, the message you were asked in, the file a task needs. You decide which apps, and how much of each.

## Connect an app

1. In Ferrin, open **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Pick the app and **sign in** to it. There are no keys to copy and nothing to install.
3. Choose what Ferrin can read, like which calendars, channels or folders. Everything else stays out of it.

## What Ferrin can see and change

Ferrin reads only what you choose, and uses it to plan.

It changes very little in your apps:

- **Calendar holds.** If your plan includes them, Ferrin adds time to your calendar for your tasks and moves it when plans change.
- **Email flags.** When you check off a task that came from a flagged or starred email, Ferrin clears the flag. See [Flagged email](/connect/flagged-email).

Ferrin doesn't send messages, reply to emails or edit your files.

## Disconnect an app

In **Settings → Connectors**, choose the app and disconnect it. Ferrin stops using it straight away. Tasks it already made stay in your plan.

## Available apps

| Category | Apps | What Ferrin does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Calendar** | Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Apple Calendar | Finds free time around your events, and holds it for your tasks |
| **Email** | Outlook Mail, Gmail, Apple Mail | Turns flagged or starred emails into tasks, with the message attached |
| **Chat** | Slack, Microsoft Teams | Turns what you're asked into a task, with who asked |
| **Files** | Google Drive, Dropbox | Finds the file a task needs and attaches it |
| **AI assistants** | Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok | Read your plan or add to it. See [Connect an AI agent](/connect/ai-agents) |
| **Health** | COROS | Plans around your training, and keeps rest days lighter |

## Which plans include connectors

- **Free** works without the cloud, so it includes the two connectors that work on your device: **Apple Calendar**, and **Apple Mail** on your Mac.
- **Paid plans** add the rest, since they run through Ferrin's servers. How many depends on the plan: see [pricing](https://www.ferrin.app/pricing).
