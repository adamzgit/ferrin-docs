Flag an email the way you already do, and it becomes a task in Ferrin, with the message attached and the details filled in. Check off the task, and the flag clears in your inbox.

| Email app | How you mark an email |
| --- | --- |
| **Outlook** | Flag it |
| **Gmail** | Star it |
| **Apple Mail** | Flag it, on your Mac |

## Set it up

1. In Ferrin, open **Settings → Connectors** and pick your email app.
2. **Sign in** to your email account.
3. Ferrin offers to bring in the emails you've already flagged. Choose that, or start from now.

Emails you've already flagged go into your **Flagged email** list, not into Today, so you can sort them when you're ready.

## What each task includes

- **A title** for what's actually asked, not the subject line.
- **The email**: who sent it, what they said and any attachments. Open it or reply straight from the task.
- **The details**: a date from the email, like "let me know by Thursday", the sender as the person on the task, and a project picked from what it's about.

New flagged emails land in your **Flagged email** list. Ones with a date also show in Today and Upcoming on that day.

## Done, unflagged and disconnected

- **Check off the task** and the flag clears in your inbox.
- **Unflag the email** and its task goes too, if you haven't changed it. If you've added to the task in Ferrin, like a date, a note or a project, it stays. Ferrin never deletes your work.
- **Disconnect** your email in **Settings → Connectors**. Tasks already made stay in your plan.

## What Ferrin reads

Ferrin only reads the emails you flag or star, and only in the accounts you connect. The rest of your inbox stays out of it.

## Plans

**Apple Mail** works on your Mac on every plan, Free included. **Outlook** and **Gmail** need a paid plan: see [pricing](https://www.ferrin.app/pricing).
