Microsoft To Do's apps retire on June 30, 2027. If you're moving to Ferrin, you can bring your lists with you and keep the habits you rely on, like flagging emails.

<Info>
  The importer arrives when Ferrin launches. [Join the waitlist](https://www.ferrin.app/why-ferrin#microsoft-to-do) and we'll tell you the day you can bring your lists over.
</Info>

## What comes over

The importer brings everything over in one step, on every plan, Free included:

- **Each list** becomes a project in Ferrin.
- **Each task** keeps its due date, its reminders and its notes.

Your lists in To Do aren't changed, so you can check both side by side before you switch.

## Where your To Do habits live in Ferrin

| In Microsoft To Do | In Ferrin |
| --- | --- |
| **My Day** | **Today**: the tasks due today, plus the ones you pick |
| **Planned** | **Upcoming**: your week, with tasks placed around your calendar |
| **Flagged email** | A **Flagged email** list: connect Outlook and every email you flag shows up there |
| **Lists** | **Projects**, as many as you like on every plan |
| **Due dates and reminders** | The same, and you can also say them: "remind me Friday at 9" |
| **Shared lists** | Shared tasks and calendars, from the Individuals plan up |

## Keep flagging emails

If you used To Do's Flagged email list, it works the same way in Ferrin:

1. Connect **Outlook** in **Settings → Connectors**. Gmail and Apple Mail work too. Outlook and Gmail need a paid plan; Apple Mail works on your Mac on every plan, Free included.
2. Ferrin offers to bring in the emails you've already flagged. They go into your **Flagged email** list, not into Today, so you can sort them when you're ready.
3. Flag an email and it becomes a task, with the message attached. Check off the task and the flag clears in your inbox.

If you unflag an email and haven't changed its task, the task goes too. If you've added to the task in Ferrin, like a date, a note or a project, it stays. More in [Flagged email](/connect/flagged-email).

## What's different

- **Ferrin is for Mac and iPhone.** Windows and Android are coming; there's no web app yet.
- **Free works on your Mac and your iPhone,** with your tasks in sync and backed up. Outlook and Gmail flags and sharing need a paid plan. See [pricing](https://www.ferrin.app/pricing).
- **You can talk to it.** Say what's on your mind, and Ferrin sorts it into tasks with the dates and people filled in. See [How Say it works](/get-started/how-say-it-works).
