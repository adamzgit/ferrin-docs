**Say it** is how you add things to Ferrin. Instead of typing tasks one by one, you say what's on your mind, half-finished thoughts and tangents included, and Ferrin turns it into tasks.

## How to add tasks with Say it

1. Press **⌥ Space** on your Mac, or use the **Action Button** or **Lock Screen** on your iPhone.
2. Say what's on your mind, the way it comes.
3. Let Ferrin sort it. It listens to the whole thought and separates the distinct tasks in it, each with the details it heard: dates, people, priority and project.
4. Check what Ferrin heard, make a quick correction if needed, then add the tasks together. See [Review before it lands](/plan/review).

## One thought, several tasks

One ramble can become several tasks. For example:

> "Remind me to call the vet about Juniper's checkup tomorrow morning, and I need to send Jane the Q3 deck before Friday, oh, and book a thirty minute walk every weekday at noon."

Ferrin finds the three tasks in that sentence and fills in what you said about each one.
