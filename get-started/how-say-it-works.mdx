---
title: "How Say it works"
description: "Say a thought the way it comes. Ferrin sorts it into tasks."
---

**Say it** is how you add things to Ferrin. Instead of typing tasks one by one, you say what's on your mind, half-finished thoughts and tangents included.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say it however it comes">
    Press ⌥ Space on your Mac, or use the Action Button or Lock Screen on your iPhone, and talk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ferrin sorts it into tasks">
    Ferrin listens to the whole thought and separates the distinct tasks in it. One ramble can become several tasks, each with the details it heard: dates, people, priority and project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it and add it">
    You see what Ferrin heard before anything is added. Make a quick correction if needed, then add the tasks together. See [Review before it lands](/plan/review).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## An example

> "Remind me to call the vet about Juniper's checkup tomorrow morning, and I need to send Jane the Q3 deck before Friday, oh, and book a thirty minute walk every weekday at noon."

Ferrin turns that into three tasks:

| Task | Details Ferrin filled in |
| --- | --- |
| Call the vet about Juniper's checkup | Tomorrow, 9:00 AM |
| Send Jane the Q3 deck | Friday, high priority, with Jane |
| Walk, 30 min | Repeats weekdays, 12:00 PM |
